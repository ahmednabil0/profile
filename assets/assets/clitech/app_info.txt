app_breif:
Clitech is an advanced, enterprise-grade mobile ecosystem engineered for healthcare practitioners to streamline and remotely oversee clinical workflows. Designed with a modular, highly scalable infrastructure, the platform integrates real-time synchronization, intelligent location services, and automated data processing. By leveraging integrated AI capabilities, Clitech automates patient data categorization, optimizes operational decision-making, and Bridges the gap between traditional clinical workflows and modern, data-driven healthcare automation.

technologies:
State Management	flutter_bloc, equatable
Backend Integration	Firebase (firebase_core, firebase_auth, cloud_firestore, firebase_storage)
Network Client	dio (REST API Consumption)
Architectural Utilities	get_it, dartz, freezed, build_runner
UI/UX Infrastructure	flutter_screenutil (Dynamic Scaling), animated_custom_dropdown, carousel_slider, easy_date_timeline
Hardware & Native APIs	Maps_flutter, geolocator, permission_handler, qr_code_scanner
Document/Media Engine	pdf, printing, flutter_pdfview, screenshot, flutter_svg
Localization & Assets	flutter_localization (Multi-language Support: Arabic/English), jiffy
